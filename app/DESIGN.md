# Seervi Portal V2 - Geologica Blue

## Creative North Star: 'Modern Geologic'
A design system that feels as stable as bedrock but as fluid as a digital stream. High contrast, extreme legibility, and a vibrant professional blue palette.

## Colors
- **Primary (Vibrant Blue):** #1c5ee3. Use for all main actions.
- **Secondary (Sunlit Gold):** #e3e01c. Use for accents and highlights.
- **Accent (Electric Green):** #1ce323. Use for success states.
- **Background:** #e8f0fd (Light Blue Tint).
- **Surface:** #ffffff (Pure White).

## Typography
- **Font Family:** Geologica (Sans-serif, wide, geometric).
- **H1:** 67px, Bold.
- **H2:** 50px, Bold.
- **Body:** 16px, Regular.

## Shapes
- **Cards:** 24px radius (Large, friendly corners).
- **Buttons:** 16px radius (Medium rounded).
- **Inputs:** 12px radius.

## Navigation
- **Header:** Sticky, white background, #1c5ee3 logo/text.
- **Footer:** Bottom navigation with #1c5ee3 active states.
